Welcome to Sea Monster!

Our company’s purpose is to provide financial support to independent game developers who may not have access to traditional funding opportunities. For the past 12 years, we have worked to help creators bring original and innovative game ideas to life by connecting them with a community of supporters. Through our platform, developers are able to showcase their projects, raise the funds needed for development, and engage directly with backers who share an interest in independent gaming. Over time, our company has contributed to the growth of the independent game industry by promoting creativity, supporting diverse voices, and helping transform early-stage concepts into completed, playable games.
